## Paste this into ASC

```
Walk into a restaurant on Martha's Vineyard and ask the table next to you: what's good here?

That's the app.

What's Good Here is a map-first food guide for the island. We don't rank restaurants — we rank dishes. The lobster roll at one place can be the best on the Vineyard while the chowder next door wins. People here know the difference. Now you do too.

Open the app, switch to map mode, and see what's worth ordering near you — pinned by category, ranked by people who actually ate it. Tap a dish to read what folks said. Save the ones you want to come back for. Rate as you go. Over time the app learns your taste and stops wasting yours.

What's inside:

• Map-first discovery — pins are dishes, not just places
• Crowd-sourced ratings, 1 to 10 — not five-star theater
• Search by craving — "fried clams," "iced coffee," "scallops" — and see who does it best nearby
• The Locals' Picks — what islanders actually order, curated by name
• Your food life — your ratings, your favorites, your taste profile. Yours. Private until you choose to share.

What we won't do:

• Pretend a generic algorithm knows the Vineyard
• Sell your data or your reviews
• Show you fifty mediocre options ranked by who paid for placement

Currently covering Martha's Vineyard. Nantucket and Cape Cod are next.

If you're visiting for a weekend or you've been coming back for forty summers, you should know what's actually good here.
```

## Notes for Dan before pasting

- **"forty summers"** — borrowed cadence from MV vernacular. Swap to "thirty," "fifty," or whatever lands right if it feels off.
- **"Five-star theater"** — direct shot at Yelp/Google Reviews. Confident, on-brand for the wedge. If you want to soften, change to "not arbitrary stars."
- **"Wasting yours"** — deliberately leaves "time" implied. If it reads weird, swap to "wasting your time."
- **Locals' Picks line** — assumes the feature is shipping at launch. If the curator names aren't ready, swap to: `• The Locals' Picks — what islanders actually order`.
- **Closing line** — strongest emotional beat. Don't lose it.

## What I deliberately did NOT include

- Pricing language ("free!") — App Store already shows price; redundant
- Feature parity claims (vs Beli/Yelp) — competitor name-checks weaken; the wedge sells itself
- "Memorial Day launch" — use **Promotional Text** field for that, not description (description can't be edited without re-review)
- Social-network framing — per memory, "this is your food life, not social features." Stayed off it.

## Alternate openings if the question/answer rhythm feels off
